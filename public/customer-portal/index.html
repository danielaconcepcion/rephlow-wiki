    4 · Next steps

    From request to contract

    1. Technical review

      You share flow, phosphorus load and discharge conditions; we check the fit with your treatment line.

      Now
    2. Stream characterisation

      Your samples are fractionated by phosphorus form, which sets the enzyme panel and the performance fee.

      Before any agreement
    3. Host-site pilot

      One unit installed in bypass and operated by rePhlow for twelve months. The pilot measures capsule lifetime, the assumption our economics depend on most.

      Phase 3 · from 2028
    4. Service contract or purchase

      Continue as a service, or buy modules once serial manufacture and a performance record exist.

      Phase 4 · from mid-2029
    5 · Request a technical review

    Tell us about your site

    Nothing is committed here. A request opens a technical review of your stream; any pilot agreement is signed separately.

    Demonstration portal built for the iGEM 2026 wiki of Madrid-UCM. No order is placed, no payment is taken and no data leaves this page. rePhlow is a university research project at TRL 2–3, not a company selling equipment.
